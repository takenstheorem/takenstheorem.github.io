VOTE STARTS 9/16

Vote will take place on Snapshot here. If you wish to increase voting power, CORPUS can be collected till the proposal deploys.

Proposal 1 // Induction

Proposal 1 determines the strongest induction. When I deployed CORPUS, I hid a meaningful opposition often found in communities: power from centralization vs. decentralization. Should the strongest induction on CORPUS come from four oligarchs who can call an influence() function on chain? Or a collective gm() call open to any CORPUS holder? These functions alter the visual for a period of time, and proposal 1 will decide which of these forces will have the greatest impact.

See the CORPUS about page for details about these "oligarchs." They are fully on chain and ERC-721 1/1s. I am gifting each oligarch token to a prior collector. (SuperRare calls these "LENS" tokens because they also fluctuate with CORPUS's Liquid Edition.)

See the oligarchs on OpenSea.

Proposal 2 // Adaptation

Proposal 2 asks how CORPUS should adapt: conserve its current stable dynamic, or tune it toward greater chaos and exploration in response to market activity. The winning options will retune the living visual. The bots hold much of the supply, so they may vote too. If you vote for adaptation, this proposal will let CORPUS fluctuate more wildly, exploring the range of visual embodiment I had built into it.

Shortly after the vote, the final renderer will be set. Fully on chain. Resonant with a market determined by the CORPUS collective.
